What to watch

The nearest candle usually tips the furthest and goes out first. If you raise the humidity, the corona weakens and the flames survive farther into the rack.

Blue streamers represent the ion-rich electric wind leaving the dome.
Bright flames are still burning but are being pushed over by the wind.
Gray wisps indicate the flame has gone out and only smoke remains.
